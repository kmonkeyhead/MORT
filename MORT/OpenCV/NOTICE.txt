OpenCV-derived C# operations in MORT
====================================
Upstream: OpenCV 4.14.0
Commit: 0654a42e19215ef25b1d367d822f3c630447e7c7
Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv/tree/4.14.0
License: https://opencv.org/license/

NavierStokesInpaint.cs is a direct C# adaptation of the 8-bit three-channel
Navier-Stokes branch of modules/photo/src/inpaint.cpp, specialized to radius 2.
MORT modifications: reusable flat state/time/result arrays, reusable stable
binary heap, four-byte working pixels, precomputed radius-2 disk offsets,
SSE4.1 channel-parallel arithmetic with a scalar fallback.
The original scanning order, priority ties, border indexing and single/double
precision arithmetic are preserved.

BackgroundRestorer.cs uses the gray conversion coefficients and Otsu arithmetic
from modules/imgproc/src/color_rgb.simd.hpp, color.simd_helpers.hpp and thresh.cpp.
The OCR mask pipeline and SIMD rectangular min/max implementation are MORT code.
No code from the previously evaluated translator is included.
